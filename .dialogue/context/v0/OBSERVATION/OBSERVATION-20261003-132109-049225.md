Installed EmDash 1.1.0 source explicitly adds @libsql/kysely-libsql and pg to its Node-native ssr.external list. That explicit dialect exclusion takes precedence over adding ssr.noExternal alone, leaving the imported dialect outside the bundle and preventing the desired web-client resolution. A noExternal-only approach was therefore insufficient; the final workaround removes only the dialect from explicit externalisation while preserving pg and other exclusions.
