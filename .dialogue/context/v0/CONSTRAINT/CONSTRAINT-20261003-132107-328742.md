Technical deployment constraint: Vercel function filesystems and /tmp must not substitute for durable production content or uploads. Turso and S3 provide the chosen durable stores. Function-local memory and temporary filesystem state also cannot reliably share admin sessions across instances. Content and media survival across redeployment/cold starts remains a live acceptance check, not something established by a successful build.
