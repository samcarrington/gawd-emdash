The reviewed Vercel documentation gives a 4.5 MB function payload limit. EmDash's signed browser-to-S3 PUT avoids sending the upload body through the function, but this does not establish that large downloads, transformations or private media proxying work. Acceptance requires a small image and a file larger than 4.5 MB, observed direct S3 upload, completion/processing, variants, download and deletion, plus expired/unauthorised/missing upload failures and approved-origin-only CORS. Large-media delivery must be tested separately; CloudFront is a possible later remedy, not an adopted implementation. Source: https://vercel.com/docs/functions/limitations.
